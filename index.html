02 / Partnership concept

Remote small-business AI pilot

A proposed 90-day pilot for U.S. small businesses, exploring how digital delivery can complement community-based AI implementation. I would welcome the opportunity to collaborate with OpenWorks or an existing U.S.-based partner, subject to eligibility and approval.

Participants would receive practical, complimentary assistance using selected capabilities of AI Profit Hunter and BORE. The goal is to deliver meaningful standalone outcomes while evaluating an adaptable approach to AI adoption.

Commercial transparency: Any paid continuation after the pilot would be entirely optional, separately contracted and subject to explicit program approval. Participants would keep the useful outputs delivered during the free pilot, without an obligation to subscribe.

01

Days 1–30 · Discover

Remote consultations, live workshops, business needs assessment and agreement on baseline measures.

02

Days 31–60 · Implement

Selected AI workflows, guided demonstrations, practical reports and human-reviewed recommendations.

03

Days 61–90 · Evaluate

Review outputs and measured changes, hand over useful results, and document lessons with participant consent.

04 / About me

Building AI that businesses can use.

I am an independent AI solutions developer and entrepreneur based in Stockholm, Sweden. My work focuses on translating AI capabilities into practical, reusable applications, especially for smaller businesses that may not have dedicated technical teams.

My approach is to start with a concrete business problem, build a focused solution, evaluate its outputs and improve it through testing and feedback. I am particularly interested in remote implementation, transparent reporting and responsible human oversight.

My current priority is to develop and validate AI Profit Hunter and BORE through real-world pilot opportunities. I am open to international collaboration, including working with local implementation partners.
